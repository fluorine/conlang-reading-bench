# Tier 4: matched zero-hint model comparison

**Update:** The site now uses a uniform [final-array regrade](FINAL-ARRAY-REGRADE.md) across the saved model responses, including Claude Sonnet. The original strict results in this report remain unchanged for provenance.

The [48-story zero-hint candidate](TIER4-ZERO-CONFIRM.md) passed its predeclared checks for Luna low. We then tested Luna medium and GPT-6.1 Terra medium on exactly the same stories and prompts, in independent fresh contexts, with an 8,192-token completion cap. The one-hint and fully disclosed stages are separate fresh requests; this experiment does not measure learning across turns. Accuracy is correct answers divided by 192 (48 stories × four questions).

| Model and effort | Zero suffix meanings | One meaning | All ten meanings | English control, initial |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| GPT-6 Luna (low) | 75/192 (39.1%) | 96/192 (50.0%) | 184/192 (95.8%) | 189/192 (98.4%) |
| GPT-6 Luna (medium) | 176/192 (91.7%) | 168/192 (87.5%) | 190/192 (99.0%) | 192/192 (100%) |
| GPT-6.1 Terra (medium) | 191/192 (99.5%) | 191/192 (99.5%) | 192/192 (100%) | 192/192 (100%) |

English controls at one and ten meanings were 192/192 and 189/192 for Luna low, 189/192 and 191/192 for Luna medium, and 191/192 and 192/192 for Terra medium. English does not contain conlang meanings; these columns denote independent attempts. A higher score with fewer hints can occur because each level is a separate request and model responses vary.

On zero-hint answers, Luna medium exceeded Luna low by **52.6 percentage points** (paired 95% story bootstrap +38.5 to +65.6), and Terra medium exceeded Luna low by **60.4 points** (+49.0 to +71.9). Intervals resample entire stories rather than treating their four questions as independent. The two Luna entries use the same model alias with different reasoning effort; effort is part of the measured configuration. These are conditional comparisons on this sample and do not isolate effort from all other sources of provider or sampling variation.

At zero hints, the initial-holder shortcut scored 31/192 (16.1%) and last-name guessing scored 43/192 (22.4%). Luna low's score passed the preregistered thresholds on this sample, but its descriptive 95% story interval is 27.6%–50.5%, spanning the 40% target. Terra is near the ceiling, so this tier provides little resolution among models near its level. The stories and grammar are public, model aliases can change, the sample is limited, and the benchmark measures this task rather than general intelligence.

Each configuration made 288 requests. Luna low cost approximately **$0.13751**, Luna medium **$0.22234**, and Terra medium **$0.47652** at recorded token-valued rates. Costs exclude any billing differences such as caching or provider adjustments; recorded standard rates were $0.10/$0.50 per million input/output tokens for Luna and $1.00/$5.00 for Terra ([OpenAI pricing](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing)). No Luna low or Terra responses were empty or capped. One Luna medium conlang response at the one-hint stage was empty after reaching the 8,192-token completion cap; it is scored incorrect and was not selectively rerun. At zero hints, missing or invalid answers were 32/192 for Luna low, 8/192 for Luna medium and 0/192 for Terra; at one hint they were 20/192, 16/192 and 0/192. All full-grammar answers parsed.
